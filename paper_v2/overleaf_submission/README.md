# Conference Manuscript Revision

Main file: `main_review.tex`.

Rendered manuscript: `main_review.pdf` (seven A4 pages).

This revision retains the accepted title and authors. It corrects metric definitions, clarifies the dataset-based H=20 cap and visit-step transitions, reports identifiable sensitivity experiments, and limits conclusions to simulated management decisions. The simulator and archived main results are unchanged.

`camera_ready_metrics.json` records the source hash and summaries recomputed from the archived ten-seed results. Overall accuracy is episode-weighted, whereas macro accuracy gives equal weight to the three intake stages within each seed.

## Visit-Count Audit

With an authorized local dataset, run in PowerShell:

```powershell
./audit_visit_horizon.ps1 -Dataset 'PATH/TO/ADNI_POMDP_Complete.csv'
```

The audit reads the CSV without modifying it. `camera_ready_visit_audit.json` contains aggregate results and the input hash only. Maximum records per participant are 20 before diagnosis/MMSE exclusion and 19 afterwards. This supports the original choice of H=20 but does not establish an optimal horizon or a ten-year period. The preprocessing notebook pools consecutive records without elapsed-time normalization.

## Sensitivity Evidence

`../../julia_alzheimer_pomdp/sensitivity_analysis.jl` and its archived JSON support the reward perturbations. They use 1,000 episodes, seed 42, and a five-decision cap, distinct from the main benchmark.

`../../julia_alzheimer_pomdp/sensitivity_cdr_raw.jl` and `sensitivity_cdr_output.txt` support the CDR MCI-row perturbation, with 10,000 episodes, seed 42, and a 20-decision cap. These single-seed checks do not validate the observation models or establish invariant policy rankings.

Restricted ADNI data, private reviewer correspondence, and conference account details are not included in this bundle.
